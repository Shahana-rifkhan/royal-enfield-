About Us

More than a showroom,
a home for riders

Welcome to Royal Enfield Sri Lanka, the official and authorised dealer for Royal Enfield motorcycles, genuine spare parts, and accessories in Sri Lanka.

Built around a passion for riding, adventure, and the legendary Royal Enfield lifestyle, we proudly bring these iconic motorcycles to riders across the island while delivering trusted service and unforgettable experiences on and off the road.

We believe motorcycles are more than just transportation — they represent freedom, exploration, connection, and the spirit of adventure. That belief drives everything we do.

From new motorcycle sales and genuine spare parts to rentals, guided tours, servicing, and rider support, our goal is to create a complete Royal Enfield experience for every customer.

Whether you are a local enthusiast, a first-time owner, or an international traveller exploring Sri Lanka on two wheels, our team is here to help you ride with confidence.

Why Us

Why riders choose us

Royal Enfield is more than a motorcycle brand, it is a lifestyle and a community built around exploration and passion for the open road. That same spirit lives within everything we do.

A Shared Journey Built on Trust

We focus on creating more than simple transactions. Every motorcycle we deliver, every tour we organise, and every rider we support is part of a larger journey built on trust, service, and unforgettable experiences.

From helping riders choose the right motorcycle to supporting adventures across Sri Lanka, our team is committed to making every interaction personal, professional, and memorable.

Built Around Adventure

Our rental and touring experiences are designed for riders who want to experience the true beauty of Sri Lanka. Ride through misty mountains, tea plantations, ancient cities, tropical coastlines, and winding countryside roads with the confidence of expert support behind you.

Selected tours include experienced guides, technical assistance, backup support, and carefully planned routes to ensure every journey is smooth, exciting, and unforgettable.

More Than a Business - A Riding Community

We are proud to be a meeting point for riders, travellers, and Royal Enfield enthusiasts from around the world. Our community is built on shared stories, unforgettable rides, new friendships, and a passion for adventure that connects people beyond borders.

Every ride creates memories. Every journey tells a story. And every rider becomes part of the Royal Enfield Sri Lanka experience.
